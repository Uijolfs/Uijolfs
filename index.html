About

From physical intuition
to physiological questions.

I'm an undergraduate studying electrical and computer engineering and physics at WPI. My research interests center on computational cardiac electrophysiology, ECG-based localization, and digital twins.

Physics gives me a way to understand a system. Physiology gives me meaningful questions to work on. I'm interested in bringing the two together through models that are useful beyond the simulation.

02

Research

Questions I'm working on.

Connecting electrical activity, mathematical models, and clinical problems.

01 / MODELCardiac modeling & digital twins+

How can a computational model capture the essential behavior of cardiac electrical propagation?

My work includes cellular automata models and simulations linking cardiac activation to ECG signals.

02 / LOCATEECG-based localization+

What can a small set of ECG measurements tell us about the location of an electrical source?

I study computational approaches to pace mapping, using the relationship between spatial position and ECG features.

03 / NAVIGATEComputational catheter navigation+

How can anatomical information and electrical measurements help guide the next step?

I explore geometry-aware approaches to navigation on cardiac surfaces.

Descriptions here are an overview. Code and publications will be linked as they become available for public release.
